About MIL Academy

A learning hub built for Media and Information Literacy education.

Our Purpose

MIL Academy supports educators and learners in developing the critical skills needed to navigate today’s complex information environment. Drawing on frameworks from UNESCO and leading media literacy organizations, the site offers structured courses, curated video resources, and assessments designed to promote genuine understanding.

What You’ll Find

  • → Courses — Progressive modules covering foundations, source evaluation, AI-era challenges, and digital citizenship.
  • → Videos — Embeds from Crash Course Media Literacy, UNESCO, and civic education partners, ready for classroom or independent use.
  • → Quizzes — Timed, randomized, sequential assessments that discourage simple lookup or sharing of answer keys.

On “Cheating-Proof” Quizzes

No web-based quiz is completely immune to determined cheating. Our design prioritizes features that raise the cost of shortcutting and reward preparation:

  • Question order and answer choices are shuffled on every attempt
  • Only one question is shown at a time; previous answers cannot be revisited
  • A continuous timer auto-submits when time expires
  • Basic deterrents against casual copy-paste and right-click

For high-stakes grading, pair these quizzes with in-person proctoring, oral follow-ups, or other academic integrity measures. The goal is authentic learning, not surveillance.

For Educators

This site is intentionally lightweight and self-contained. You can host the files on any static web server, customize the course descriptions, expand the question banks in js/quiz-data.js, or add your own YouTube embeds. No database or backend is required for basic use.
